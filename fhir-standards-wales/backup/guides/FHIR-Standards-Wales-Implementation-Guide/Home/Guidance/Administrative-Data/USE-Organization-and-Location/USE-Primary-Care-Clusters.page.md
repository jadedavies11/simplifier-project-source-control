<span style="font-size: 0.95em;">The delivery of primary care to the population of Wales is organized via primary care clusters that cover the population of a defined geographical area. Some clusters just cover general practice, but others may include more comprehensive coverage of primary care organizations, as in the theoretical example below.</span>

<span style="font-size: 0.95em;">As illustrated, a separate OrganizationAffiliation resource is used for each member organization.  Every cluster is managed as part of a specific health board.</span>
