<span style="font-size: 0.95em;">The {{pagelink:DataStandardsWales-Location, text: Location}} resource contains information about a physical or virtual place where services are provided and resources and participants may be stored, found, contained, or accommodated. Typical examples include buildings, wards, corridors, rooms or beds, but may also include mobile clinics, a vehicle or lift, or a patient's home. Location resources can contain details about physical address and position, location form and type and times of operation.</span>
