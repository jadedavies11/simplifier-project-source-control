<span style="font-size: 0.95em;">A clinician is a unique entity with potentially multiple identifiers and other attributes that relate to them as a person. These aspects are all captured by the {{pagelink:DataStandardsWales-Practitioner, text: Practitioner}} resource. When a clinician performs a role during a patient event, it is often important to know the capacity within which they act. This information relates to their employment by a healthcare organisation, including aspects such as role and speciality which are captured in a {{pagelink:DataStandardsWales-PractitionerRole, text: PractitionerRole}} resource.</span>

<span style="font-size: 0.95em;">This worked example illustrates a qualified dietitian who works for Hywel Dda University Health Board as a specialist diabetes dietitian, while also providing more general dietitian services in Swansea Bay University Health Board.</span>
