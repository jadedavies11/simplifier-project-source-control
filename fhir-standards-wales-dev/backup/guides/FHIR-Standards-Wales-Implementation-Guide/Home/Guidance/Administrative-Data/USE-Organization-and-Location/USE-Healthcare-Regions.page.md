<span style="font-size: 0.95em;">A region is an administrative grouping of health boards for the purpose of collaborative delivery of healthcare services to their combined populations, where appropriate. Health boards can participate in multiple regions.  The membership model for the South Central Wales Region involves four OrganizationAffiliation resources, one for each participating health board.</span>
