<span style="font-size: 0.95em;">The {{pagelink:DataStandardsWales-OrganizationAffiliation, text: OrganizationAffiliation}} resource supports formal and enduring one-to-many and many-to-many relationships between organisations, that fall outside of the strict organisation hierarchy. Each OrganizationAffiliation resource links a pair of organisations. One organisation can be considered to own the arrangement, while the other is a participating organisation. The relationship can optionally include details of the involved locations and healthcare services of the participating organisation.</span>
