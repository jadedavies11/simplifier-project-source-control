<span style="font-size: 0.95em;">The {{pagelink:DataStandardsWales-Organization, text: Organization}} resource contains information about health and care organisations. In Wales this may include health boards and NHS trusts, hospitals, dental practices, GP practices and primary care clusters, but could be any public, private or voluntary sector organisation whose activities encompass the funding or provision of health care services. To aid Organization recognition and retrieval, the `Organization.type` element carries structured information defining the sector, domain, classification and subclassification. Each `alias` can also be classified using an `aliasType`extension.</span>

* <span style="font-size: 0.95em;">An encounter record for an admission or outpatient consultation will refer to the organisation that provided the care.</span>
* <span style="font-size: 0.95em;">A patient demographic record may refer to the GP practice with which they are registered and the health board responsible for their care (based on their postcode of residence).</span>
